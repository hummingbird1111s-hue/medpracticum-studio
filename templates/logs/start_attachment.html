💡 Why add your competency areas?

Your competency areas are the “target skills” for this attachment. Adding them makes your report sharper and faster to build:

  • AI drafts each chapter around the skills you're supposed to learn, not just your logs.
  • Lessons Learnt, Challenges, and Recommendations only surface what matters to your course.
  • Supervisors see clear evidence you covered every required skill.
  • You can always add or edit them later from All Attachments.
