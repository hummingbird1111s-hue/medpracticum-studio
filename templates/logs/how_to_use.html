Welcome to Your User Guide

Everything you need to know about navigating MedPracticum Studio, from logging your first day to downloading your final report and presentation.

1. Setup 2. Daily Logs 3. Reports & PPT 4. AI Assistant

1 Setting Up Your Attachment

Start / Edit Attachment

Before you can add logs, you must create an attachment session. This tells the system where you are working and when.

  • Enter the facility name, district, and type (e.g., District Hospital).
  • Set your exact start and end dates.
  • Add your clinical supervisor's name.
  • Optional: You can add specific "Competency Areas" so the AI knows exactly what skills you want to focus on.

2 Recording Your Daily Logs

New Daily Log

This is where you record what you did each day. The more detail you provide here, the better your final report will be.

  • Select the date and the specific hospital unit (e.g., OPD, RCH).
  • Activity Summary: Briefly describe what you did (e.g., "Registered 15 new patients into the LHIMS system").
  • Skills & Challenges: Note any new skills learned or problems encountered (and how you solved them).
  • Use the Tags and Tallies to quickly count things like "NHIS Claims Processed".

3 Generating Your Outputs

Reports Page

This page compiles all your logs into a full academic report (PDF & Word).

  • Click Generate Official Report to let the AI read all your logs and draft the chapters.
  • You can edit the generated text manually, change the cover logo, and even adjust the font size and spacing.
  • Download the final result as a PDF or a Word Document.

Presentations Page

Creates a PowerPoint presentation from your logs, perfect for your final defense.

  • Choose from 16 different templates (including the official HIM template).
  • Preview your cover slide live before building.
  • The AI writes short bullet points and even adds Speaker Notes to help you present.
  • Download the `.pptx` file or a PDF version of the slides.

4 Your AI Assistant (🩺)

The Floating Doctor Icon

Look for the floating 🩺 icon at the bottom right of your screen. You can drag it anywhere. Click it to open a conversation with the AI.

  • Ask for help: "Write an introduction paragraph for my report."
  • Analyze data: "How many logs have I recorded so far?"
  • Vision (Images): Click the 📎 paperclip to upload a photo of a document or whiteboard, and ask the AI to "Transcribe this" or "Explain this".

5 Other Important Tools

All Attachments

If you do multiple rotations (e.g., one at a District Hospital, another at a Teaching Hospital), you can manage them all here.

View Logs

A full timeline of every daily log you've submitted. You can edit or delete specific entries here.

Workshops

Record any official training, orientations, or continuing education sessions you attended.

Archive

Accidentally deleted a log or an attachment? Don't worry. Deleted items go here, and you can restore them with one click.
