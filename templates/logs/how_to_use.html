Everything in MedPracticum Studio builds on top of an attachment — the record of where you are doing your practicum. You need one before you can add any logs or generate a report.

What to fill in
  • Facility name — e.g. "Bolgatanga Regional Hospital"
  • District & region — where the facility is located
  • Start & end dates — the exact period of your attachment
  • Clinical supervisor — the staff member who signs off your logbook
  • Competency areas — optional, but strongly recommended. The AI uses these to shape the whole report.
💡
Pro tip: List 3–6 competency areas (e.g. "patient registration", "medical coding", "data quality control"). The AI will weave them into the Introduction, Skills, Lessons, and Recommendations sections.
Try it now

Daily logs are the raw material for everything the AI writes. Each log should cover one day you actually worked — what you did, what you learned, and any problems you hit.

What makes a strong log
  • Unit / department — OPD, RCH, Records, Laboratory, etc.
  • Activity summary — 1–3 sentences describing what you did
  • Skills acquired — new things you learned that day
  • Challenges faced — problems you encountered (and how you solved them)
  • Tags & tallies — quick counters like "NHIS claims processed: 12"
Good vs weak example
✅ "Registered 15 new outpatients into LHIMS, verified NHIS cards, and booked 4 follow-up appointments."
❌ "Worked at OPD."
💡
Rule of thumb: If a stranger couldn't picture your day from the log, it's too short. Aim for 40+ words per log — the AI will expand it into professional prose.

💡
Did you add new logs after generating? Just click Generate again — the app detects that your logs changed and re-drafts everything automatically, so the report always reflects your latest data.

Look for the floating 🩺 icon at the bottom-right of every page. You can drag it anywhere. Click it to open a chat with an AI that has read access to your logs, competencies, and workshops. It can also see images — attach a photo of a whiteboard, a signed form, or a handwritten record and ask it to transcribe.

Vision mode: Click the 📎 paperclip in the AI chat to attach an image, PDF, .docx, or .txt file (max 5 MB). Then ask "Transcribe this" or "Summarise this document". Perfect for turning a photo of your handwritten logbook into typed text.

Running multiple rotations (District → Teaching Hospital)? Manage them all here. Each attachment keeps its own logs, reports, and presentations.

View Logs

A full timeline of every daily log you've submitted. Edit or delete individual entries — the AI will pick up the change on your next generation.

Workshops

Record any official training, orientation, or CPD session. The AI will mention them in the Workshops section of your report and the corresponding presentation slide.

Archive

Accidentally deleted something? Everything that gets removed goes here first. One click restores it — nothing is gone forever.

⚠️
Deleting a log is soft — it moves to Archive. Deleting an attachment also moves to Archive but archives its reports too. Restore from Archive if you change your mind.
