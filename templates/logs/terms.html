Part A — Terms & Conditions

The rules for using the platform.

1 Use of Service

MedPracticum Studio is provided to help you document your clinical attachment, synthesize your daily activities, and generate academic reports.

  • You must use this platform solely for your official COHY practicum requirements.
  • You are responsible for ensuring that all data (logs, facility details, workshops) entered into the system is accurate and truthful.
  • Account sharing is strictly prohibited. Your login credentials are personal and secure.

2 AI Drafting & Capabilities

The system utilizes advanced Artificial Intelligence to synthesize your raw daily logs into professional academic prose and presentation slides.

  • AI is an assistant, not the author: The generated text is a draft based on your inputs. You are responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving the final report.
  • Data Processing: Your logs are securely sent to our AI provider solely to generate your report. We do not use your personal data to train public AI models.
  • System Limits: To ensure fair usage for all students, there are daily rate limits on AI generation. If the AI is unavailable, the system will safely fall back to template-based generation.

3 Data Privacy & Security

We take the confidentiality of your academic and clinical data seriously.

  • Your reports and daily logs are stored securely and are only accessible to you and authorized COHY faculty members.
  • No Patient Data: You must never enter real patient names, ID numbers, or sensitive medical records into your daily logs. Always anonymize your data.
  • Generated reports include a secure QR verification code to ensure academic integrity.

Part B — Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and protect your data.

7 Information We Collect

To run the platform, we collect:

  • Account details: Your name, username, email, student ID, and department as provided during registration.
  • Practicum data: Facility details, daily logs, units, skills, challenges, and workshop records you enter.
  • AI conversation data: Messages, attached files, and images you send to the AI Assistant.
  • Technical data: Browser type, device information, IP address, and timestamps used for security and debugging.
  • Session & cookies: A session cookie to keep you logged in, and a CSRF token to protect against cross-site attacks.

8 How We Use & Share Your Data

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To generate your reports, presentations, and AI drafts based on the content you submit.
  • To let authorized COHY faculty review your practicum progress.
  • To detect abuse, rate-limit AI usage, and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your data. Your logs and conversations are never used to train public AI models. We share data only with our AI provider (for the sole purpose of generating your response) and with authorized COHY faculty as required for your practicum.

9 Data Retention & Your Rights

  • Retention: Your practicum data is kept for the duration of your course and archived thereafter as required by COHY policy. AI chat history can be deleted at any time from the History panel.
  • Access: You can view, download, and edit all data you have entered.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual AI chats or all chat history from the History panel. To delete your account or permanent records, contact platform support.
  • Export: You can export your reports and logs as PDF or Word documents at any time.

10 Cookies & Session Data

MedPracticum Studio uses only strictly necessary cookies — a session cookie to keep you logged in, and a CSRF token for security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Clearing your browser cookies will log you out.

11 Contact for Privacy Concerns

If you have questions about how your data is handled, want to exercise your data rights, or believe your privacy has been compromised, contact platform support using the button below. We aim to respond within 5 working days.
