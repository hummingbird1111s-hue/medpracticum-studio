INTRODUCTION

I undertook a health-information practicum at Bolgatanga Regional Hospital, the principal referral centre for Ghana's Upper East Region, from 2 November to 29 November 2026. The attachment aimed to consolidate my competencies in patient registration, electronic record management, and data reporting.

During the month I rotated through the Records Unit, the Reproductive-Child Health Unit and the outpatient booking desk, applying LHIMS/HAMS for registration, appointment scheduling and data extraction, thereby reinforcing my skills in accurate information capture and confidentiality.

FACILITY PROFILE

Bolgatanga Regional Hospital is a regional hospital located in the Bolgatanga Municipal of the Upper East Region, Ghana.

Bolgatanga Regional Hospital serves as the main referral centre for the Upper East Region.

Vision and Mission: To provide quality secondary healthcare services to the people of the Upper East Region.

Organisational Structure: Headed by a Medical Director with departmental heads for clinical and administrative units.

ACTIVITIES PERFORMED

During the practicum period, I was involved in the following activities as recorded in my logbook:

Records Unit

In the Records Unit, I was involved in registration of new patients.

SKILLS ACQUIRED

  • Registering new patients: I accurately captured personal and demographic data for 15 patients, ensuring completeness before adding them to the electronic health information system.
3

SKILLS ACQUIRED (CONTINUED)

  • Electronic record management: I demonstrated proficiency in navigating the LHIMS/HAMS system, updating patient records securely, and maintaining strict confidentiality protocols.
  • Data reporting: I compiled weekly statistical summaries from outpatient registers, validating data accuracy and preparing the figures for departmental review.

WORKSHOPS

No formal workshops were held during this period. However, I received on-the-job training regarding the new DHIMS2 data entry protocols and participated in a departmental briefing on patient confidentiality and data protection regulations.

4

LESSONS LEARNT

  • I learnt the importance of cross-checking patient demographics to prevent duplicate medical records.
  • I realised that clear communication between clinical staff and records officers is critical for accurate data capture.
  • I appreciated the role of data security and patient confidentiality in health information management.

OBSERVATIONS

I observed that while the LHIMS/HAMS system streamlined patient registration, the high patient volume often caused bottlenecks at the records desk. I also noted that inconsistent handwriting on physical referral forms posed challenges for digital data entry.

CHALLENGES

The primary challenge I faced was the sheer volume of patients requiring registration, which sometimes led to minor delays. Additionally, adapting to the specific coding structures used in the facility's electronic system required close attention and frequent consultation with senior staff.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on my observations, I recommend the introduction of additional workstations during peak hours to reduce congestion. I also suggest organizing periodic refresher training for staff on digital data entry and patient interaction.

CONCLUSION

The practicum at Bolgatanga Regional Hospital provided me with an invaluable opportunity to apply theoretical knowledge in a real-world setting. I am now more confident in my ability to manage health information systems and look forward to contributing to the health sector.
