FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay before I can use the app?

No. You can sign up, create your attachment, and record daily logs for free. Payment is only required when you're ready to generate your first report or presentation.

How does payment work?

Payments are processed securely through Paystack, a leading payment gateway trusted by businesses across Ghana and Africa. After signing up and creating your attachment, you'll be able to check out through Paystack's secure payment page.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money, Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards, and bank transfers — all handled securely by Paystack. You never enter card details into our servers.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time payment that covers your entire practicum period. There are no recurring charges, no automatic renewals, and no hidden fees.

What is your refund policy?

If you haven't generated any reports or presentations, you can request a full refund within 7 days of payment. Once AI generation has begun, refunds are reviewed case-by-case. Contact our support team to start a refund.

Is my data safe? Do you store patient information?

Your logs and reports are encrypted and accessible only to you and authorized COHY faculty. We never store real patient data — the system requires you to anonymize all logs before submission. Every report is QR-verified for academic integrity.
